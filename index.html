Research & Deep Learning

Agentic AI Mental Health Detection, English & Bangla (B.Sc. Thesis). A bilingual AI risk-triage system that classifies mental-health-related social-media text and turns each prediction into a confidence score, a risk level, and a recommended action. BanglaBERT reached 97.54% accuracy and F1 on 7,131 Bangla posts, and MentalBERT reached 88% F1 on 11,013 English texts, both benchmarked against TF-IDF baselines.

  • MentalBERT
  • BanglaBERT
  • Transformers
  • Deep Learning
  • NLP
  • Agentic AI
Text→Preprocessing→Transformer Model→Classification→Agentic AI→Risk-Aware Support

Problem

Detecting mental-health-related signals in social-media text across two languages.

Dataset

7,131 Bangla posts and 11,013 English texts from social media.

Approach and models

Transformer-based classification with MentalBERT and BanglaBERT, benchmarked against TF-IDF baselines.

Agentic AI

Each prediction gets a High, Medium, or Low risk level and an action (allow, warn, or human review), with explainability and helpline referrals.
